ABOUT COMTRACK

Turning public concerns into visible action.

ComTrack is designed to make complaint reporting and follow-up easier for citizens. Instead of being left wondering what happens after submitting an issue, users can access a structured process for reporting concerns and checking their progress.

Our goal is to support clearer communication, organized complaint handling, and a more accessible public grievance experience.
