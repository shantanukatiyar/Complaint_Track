OUR STORY

Making complaint reporting simpler.

A clear, organised way for citizens to communicate concerns and follow their progress.

One place for raising concerns and tracking updates.

Reporting a public issue should not feel confusing. Citizens need a straightforward way to explain their concerns and understand what happens next.

ComTrack is designed around this need. It brings complaint submission, complaint tracking, and status updates together in one accessible interface.

Our goal is to support a more transparent and organised grievance-reporting experience through a simple digital platform.
