A SIMPLE PROCESS

From raising a concern to tracking its progress.

ComTrack is designed to make the complaint journey easier to understand. Follow the steps below to see how citizens can use the portal.

Start a Complaint → Explore the Process ↓
THE PROCESS

Three simple steps

A structured journey for submitting a concern and checking the information available about it.

✎
STEP 01

Submit your complaint

Open the complaint form, enter the required details, describe the issue clearly, and submit the information.

TIP Provide accurate and relevant details.
⌕
STEP 02

Track your complaint

Use the complaint reference number or the tracking details supported by the portal to look up the submitted complaint.

TIP Keep your reference details safe.
◷
STEP 03

Review status updates

Check the latest status and any available updates to understand the progress recorded for your complaint.

TIP Recheck when new updates are expected.
YOUR JOURNEY

What happens at each stage?

Understand the information involved at each point of the complaint journey.

01
SUBMISSION

Describe the issue

Provide the relevant complaint details, such as the subject, category, location if requested, and a clear description.

02
REFERENCE

Identify your complaint

If the system generates a complaint ID, save it so you can identify the correct record when using the tracking page.

03
TRACKING

Check the available status

Look for the recorded complaint status and any progress information available in the system.

04
FOLLOW-UP

Review new information

Return to the tracking page when needed to check whether the complaint record contains additional updates.

i

Before you submit

Make sure your complaint details are accurate and relevant. The availability of complaint IDs, tracking information, and status updates depends on the functionality implemented in the portal.
